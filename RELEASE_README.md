# Lexello

Lexello is a daily word puzzle game designed to challenge your vocabulary, deduction, and problem-solving skills every single day.

Everyone around the world receives the exact same daily word puzzle. Guess the mystery word in six tries or fewer, keep your daily winning streak alive, and see how your performance compares with friends and the global community.

---

## How to Play

The objective is to guess the hidden five-letter word in six attempts. Each guess must be a valid English word.

After submitting each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the solution:

- **Green**: The letter is correct and placed in the exact right position.
- **Yellow**: The letter is present in the word, but it belongs in a different position.
- **Grey**: The letter is not present in the word at all.

Use the color clues from each turn to narrow down the remaining possibilities and deduce the hidden word before running out of attempts.

---

## Key Features

### The Daily Challenge
A brand new puzzle goes live every day at midnight. Because every player receives the same word on any given calendar date, Lexello offers a fair, shared daily puzzle experience.

### Player Statistics and Streaks
Track your personal progress over time with comprehensive gameplay statistics:
- Games played and total win rate
- Current winning streak and best-ever streak record
- Guess distribution chart showing how often you solve puzzles in one, two, three, four, five, or six tries

### Clans and Social Competition
- Form a clan with friends, family, or colleagues using a unique clan invite code.
- View clan member rosters and compare average scores and streaks.
- Compete on the daily clan leaderboard to find out who solved today's puzzle in the fewest attempts.

### Global Leaderboard
Check the daily leaderboard to see the top solvers for each day's challenge, ranked by attempt count and completion speed.

### Play Instantly, Save Anytime
- **Guest Mode**: Start playing immediately without any registration or setup required. Your progress is saved directly to your device.
- **Account Protection**: Link your account with Google at any time in settings to protect your streaks, merge existing progress, and access your stats securely.

---

## Game Rules and Word Acceptance

- Words are verified against a comprehensive dictionary of recognized English words.
- Grey letters indicate letters not found in the target word, but they remain available to use if you wish to test different word structures in subsequent attempts.
- Once a daily puzzle is completed (or all six attempts are used), the puzzle ends for that day, and a countdown timer will display when the next daily challenge unlocks.
